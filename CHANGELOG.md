# Changelog

Every release of Blotto, newest first. Download any version from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/pranvirsingh/Blotto/releases).

## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-02

The first public release.

### New
- Ink-pipe puzzles: twist pipe tiles to carry the ink from Blotto's inkwell to every sleepy lamp
- Endless story reels that grow from 3×3 to 8×11 boards
- Special reels: The Negative (inverted film), Lights Out! (only tiles beside the ink are visible) and The Midnight Negative (both at once)
- Stars for solving close to par, hints earned from 3-star reels, undo, and long-press to bolt a pipe in place
- Picture Rush: solve boards against the clock, with extra time for every solve and a saved best run
- A 1930s cartoon look: film grain, scratches, flicker, iris wipes, a countdown leader and title cards
- Synthesised music and sound effects, each with its own on/off switch
